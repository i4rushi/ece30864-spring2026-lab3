(a)
Pros of Agile:

    Adaptability: Changes can be implemented at any stage of the process.

    Customer Satisfaction: Continuous delivery of useful software ensures the client is happy.

Cons of Agile:

    Scope Creep: Lack of a fixed end-date or definition can lead to ever-expanding requirements.

    Documentation: fast-paced sprints often lead to neglected or sparse documentation.

(b) 
Scrum Master vs Product Owner

    Scrum Master: Acts as a servant-leader and coach; facilitates meetings (standups, retrospectives); removes blockers for the team; ensures the Scrum process is followed.

    Product Owner: Represents the stakeholders/customers; manages and prioritizes the Product Backlog; defines the "What" (features) and maximizes the value of the product.

(c) 3 Types of Sprint Meetings (List 2 points each)

    Sprint Planning:

        Team selects items from the backlog to work on.

        Team estimates effort and defines the Sprint Goal.

    Daily Standup (Scrum):

        Time-boxed (usually 15 min) sync on progress.

        Members answer: What did I do yesterday? What will I do today? Do I have blockers?

    Sprint Review:

        Team demonstrates the "Done" increment to stakeholders.

        Feedback is gathered to update the backlog. (Alternatively: Sprint Retrospective - discuss what went well/wrong and plan improvements).

(d) Differentiate Jira Issue Types

    Epic: A large body of work that can be broken down into smaller stories (e.g., "Build Payment System").

    Story: A user requirement or feature manageable within a sprint (e.g., "As a user, I want to pay via PayPal").

    Bug: A problem or error that impairs functionality (e.g., "Submit button crashes app").

    Task: A piece of work that isn't a direct user feature but is necessary (e.g., "Update server config").
